# FitApp Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

FitApp ("the app") is a fitness and nutrition tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does with it.

**Short version:** your data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. iOS may include app data in the device backups you have enabled. If you connect a Google account, FitApp also stores a backup file in a private app folder in your own Google Drive. We (the developer) never receive your data, and the app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Data the app stores on your device

- Profile: name, age, weight, height, fitness goal, avatar emoji
- Workouts: your exercise list and workout history
- Nutrition: meals you log (calories, protein, carbs, fats), daily water intake, and the custom foods you create (name, serving size, calories and macros)
- Body tracking: your weight history and the body measurements you choose to record (for example wrist, arm or waist), with dates
- Settings: dark mode and favorite recipes

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone and is not sent to us. As part of iOS's normal device-backup behavior, Apple may include app data in an iCloud or computer backup according to your device backup settings. FitApp does not control those system backups; see [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for how Apple handles iCloud data. Deleting the app, or using **Settings → Delete All My Data**, removes it from the app on your device.

## Optional Google backup

If you tap "Connect Google Account" or "Restore from Google", the app asks you to sign in with Google and to allow access to its **own hidden app data folder** in your Google Drive (permission: `drive.appdata`). We also receive your Google account email address from Google, only to show you which account is connected.

- FitApp can read and write only its own backup file in that hidden folder. It cannot see any of your other Google Drive files.
- The backup file contains the data listed above.
- The backup is stored in your Google account. The developer has no access to it. Google processes it under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- The sign-in token is stored in your iPhone's Keychain, on this device only.
- You can stop at any time with **Settings → Disconnect Google**, delete the backup with **Settings → Delete Cloud Backup**, or remove FitApp's access in your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party apps with account access).

FitApp's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.
- We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
- We do not use your data to train AI models.

## Health information

FitApp provides general fitness and nutrition information. Calorie and nutrient goals are rough estimates and are not medical advice. Weight and body measurements are recorded only because you enter them, and are used only to show your own progress and to estimate your daily goals. FitApp does not read from or write to Apple Health.

## Children

FitApp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
